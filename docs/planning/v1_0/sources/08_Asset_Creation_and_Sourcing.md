# 08 · Asset creation, sourcing and provenance plan

**Version 1.0 · 29 October 2026 · Design handoff baseline - production acquisition pending**

This is a new product with no Mixline asset reuse. Steampunk and Android first are confirmed. The user requested comparable concept renders for A — Precision Atelier and C — Crystal Current, and named the AI assistant ParDay. Every production asset below is **proposed or to create**, unless a later manifest explicitly records acquisition, rights verification and integration. A generated concept is a review artifact, not a production asset or a completed acquisition of fonts, sample packs or stock.

Local Android delivery comes first; AWS expansion is later. Prioritize the reusable native control kit, source/download states, ParDay proposal states and authorized local demo/validation media. No cloud deployment asset or account setup is required for design. The roughly US$35/month future AWS target excludes ParDay AI charges and does not fund or authorize font, stock, audio or other asset procurement.

## 1. Asset strategy

Create original control geometry and material recipes for the app's distinctive instrument. Source coherent general-purpose icons and fonts from verified upstreams. Produce or commission demo music and samples with rights that explicitly cover application bundling and intended user output. Build test signals reproducibly. Use generated raster imagery for material exploration, illustration or marketing where appropriate; exact controls, waveforms, numerical data and diagrams require deterministic production assets.

The selected material direction is **C — Crystal Current: pale frosted glass, titanium and subtle brass**. Retain a clear instrument structure. A remains comparison history and is not selected as a night theme. The app must remain complete using its Essential visual tier. Asset failure cannot remove transport, meters, deck identity or critical status. User album artwork is cached appropriately and never substitutes for a track's textual identity. Prefer compatible open-source assets when they meet quality, maintenance and redistribution needs; paid options remain permitted after comparison, without spending being authorized by this plan.

## 3. Sourcing and rights workflow

Use the status sequence **proposed → shortlisted → acquired/created → rights verified → integrated → verified**. A catalogue listing, subscription, search result or download link is not evidence that an asset can be embedded in a commercial app. Review fonts, bundled stock, music, model weights and demos separately. Do not redistribute commercial asset source files in an agent package without the necessary permission.

Barlow's official repository declares SIL Open Font License 1.1.[1] Lucide uses ISC, with MIT terms for listed Feather-derived icons, and requires the relevant notices.[3] These facts support shortlisting; the exact package still needs a saved license and checksum. IBM Plex also declares OFL 1.1 in its official repository; the actual selected files, notice packaging and required glyphs must be checked at acquisition.[2]

For commissioned audio, agree explicitly on app distribution, user recordings/exports, demos, promotional use, modification, stems, territory, duration and any attribution. Avoid assuming “royalty-free” means unrestricted redistribution of samples as extractable assets. Keep ownership of the compositions and the recordings clear. No procurement spend or license fee is committed by this plan.

The same review applies to video, karaoke text and ParDay examples. A music licence does not automatically cover lyric display or a particular video. Provider names and marks, if shown in source selectors, must follow the applicable permitted use rather than being redrawn as implied partnerships. Do not include downloaded Spotify/YouTube music or catalogue artwork in the shipped demo pack; Document 14 defines authorized import and discovery paths. The user's broad source ambition is preserved through adapters and eligibility states, not bundled unlicensed media.

## 4. Required asset manifest

Each entry records:

`asset_id`, `name`, `role`, `status`, `owner`, `source_url`, `author`, `acquisition_date`, `license_name`, `license_reference`, `permitted_uses`, `attribution`, `modifications`, `app_redistribution_permission`, `path`, `format`, `bytes`, `dimensions_or_duration`, `checksum`, `version`, `accessible_description`, `fallback`, `review_evidence`, and `requirement_ids`.

Store editable masters separately from optimized app files. Retain provenance/license evidence with the project. Commit generated fixtures with their generator and seed. Pin source versions so an upstream replacement does not silently change visual or audio behavior. Rights-unknown assets cannot pass the release gate; use an original or appropriately licensed fallback.

## 5. Creation briefs for build agents

### Original dial family

Create a precision instrument dial for selected Crystal Current with a pale opaque or muted titanium hub, a restrained brass chamfer, a single dark-ink high-contrast index and a readable scale. Derive its low-light variant from C's semantic tokens; do not copy A's graphite recipe as the default. Deliver parameterized vector/native geometry for normal, focused, touched, centred and disabled states at several target sizes. Text remains native and separate. Demonstrate grayscale readability, Essential-tier equivalence and nonoverlapping 48dp hit areas. The dial must reflect the engine's actual value and units; a cosmetic rotation is insufficient.

### Glass material study — future image generation

“Create a material study for an original mobile DJ instrument in selected Crystal Current: three bounded pale frosted-glass panels on a mineral-light stage, brushed titanium, delicate edge highlights and restrained brass anchoring details, with stable dark-ink labels over opaque backing. Show subtle depth without refracting text. Studio lighting, front-on presentation, no logos, no tiny invented words, no music-data charts, no decorative gears or steam. This image explores visual material; it is not a functional interface.”

After concept selection, implement native recipes and fallbacks; do not ship the concept image as the control surface. AGSL requires Android 13+.[4] An equivalent static/vector tier must exist for other selected device profiles.

### A/C concept comparison — completed; C selected

The completed generation brief requested two directions using Document 04's shared composition: comparable control positions, fictional track identity, BPM/key values, framing and ParDay placement. A uses graphite, smoked glass and champagne brass; C uses pale frosted glass, titanium and restrained brass details. The exact prompts, generation date, variant identity and selected traits are recorded. A raster can be reviewed for art direction but cannot pass native touch, accessibility, engine-state or performance requirements. Do not regenerate the comparison as an outstanding task or distribute it as a finished app screenshot.

The A/C concepts have now been generated and visually reviewed; the asset register records their real paths and checksums under AS-28 as `concept_generated`. Document 04 records the visible layout differences and omissions. A/C review status and production asset readiness remain separate. Do not mark the fonts, knobs, sound packs or music shown by implication as acquired simply because a concept has been generated. The owner selected C. Preserve both generated files as the comparison record, but create new production assets for Crystal Current.

### ParDay identity and interface family

Create a small legible **ParDay** word treatment and optional abstract mechanical/optical mark that fits selected C and its high-contrast/low-light treatments. Keep a text-only fallback. Deliver neutral, selected, pending, local-only, needs-review and unavailable states. Semantic states should rely on text and shape as well as colour. Do not reuse provider logos as ParDay's identity or add an assistant face without a user benefit.

Create structured proposal components for crate changes, cue suggestions, transition previews and route explanations. Include compact and expanded layouts, long track names, unknown/source-restricted state, expired proposals, explicit review/apply, and accessible labels. No generated animation implies a successful command before acknowledgement. Model/provider names belong in understandable configuration/context, not decorative endorsement badges. Optional spoken feedback must remain a separate user choice and never become an unrequested master-output sample.

### Video and karaoke fixtures

Produce a short original video with known frame rate, synchronized original audio and a companion timed-text track with independently authored words. Include variants with delayed captions, a missing video frame segment and an unsupported fixture only in the test suite. Keep source video, audio master, caption authoring file and synchronization ground truth. Verify seeking, loop boundaries and cue preview independently. No lyrics from commercial music are needed for this validation.

### Original factory track and stems

Produce a 90–150 second original track with a verified tempo grid, clear phrases, instrumental variation and a companion deliberately drifting-tempo version. Supply full mix and independently authored stems with exact offsets, BPM/key reference and rights declaration. Use varied styles to challenge analysis, including electronic, hip-hop, soca/calypso-inspired, reggae-inspired and live drums. Do not imitate a named performer or borrow their recording. Manually verified ground truth is stored separately from automated analysis output.

### Deterministic waveform/test fixtures

Generate impulse trains and layered sine/noise tracks at the supported sample rates and channel configurations. Supply sample-indexed ground truth and expected extrema at several zoom levels. Include silence, transients, clipping, variable tempo and long-duration media. Preserve generator source, version, seed and checksum. These fixtures validate correctness; they are labelled tests rather than disguised as finished music.

### Optional later marketing concept

“Create an original product atmosphere for a handheld professional DJ instrument using selected Crystal Current: pale frosted liquid glass, titanium and restrained brass. The focus is tactile material and precise construction, with empty safe areas for later placement of real approved app captures. No invented brand, no fake store ratings, no text claims, no depiction of unsupported external controller connections.”

## 6. Packaging and budget gates

Proposed starting budgets: approximately 8 MiB of non-audio visual assets, 1–2 MiB for necessary font files, and separately downloadable or explicitly packaged demo/sample content. These numbers are planning limits to reconcile with supported languages and final distribution size. Audio fixtures used only by tests must not inflate the shipping app. No giant video, per-state 4K knob atlas or mandatory 3D scene sits behind the live surface.

For every asset, validate format, integrity, memory footprint, dimensions, fallback, accessibility, appearance at actual scale and license evidence. Audio assets additionally require channel/sample-rate metadata, headroom, loop seams, start alignment and audible review. Prompt output is only a draft; generation does not certify rights, correctness or production suitability.

Current production acquisition, native rendering, audio verification and rights status: **NOT VERIFIED / PENDING**. Concept generation/review is recorded separately when its real outputs exist. The 30-class matrix is the work order whose applicable items agents must close with evidence, not a claim that production assets already exist.

### Optional community-source distribution

Document 17 defines the proposed public repository boundary. A future source release needs redistributable fonts, icons, textures, demo media, models and fixtures, with notices and reproducible source files. Personal acquired media and paid components are not automatically redistributable. Keep nonredistributable developer assets out of public history and provide a permitted build fallback where needed. The two generated concept renders remain design references; no exclusive copyright or production-asset clearance is inferred from generation. No public asset release is authorized by this planning revision.
